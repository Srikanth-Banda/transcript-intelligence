No call-type label

Nothing says support, external or internal. A title rule misses 3 of the 70 customer calls.

Stage 0: 30 calls with no outside attendee are internal. A model reads the opening of the other 70.

An AI summary, score and topics came with the data

The provided score tracks its own sentence labels at 0.94. The topics are 351 different strings.

Kept apart, never an input to any stage. Used only to compare: my score tracks theirs at 0.96, which is consistency, not proof.

A March outage skews the timeline

Monthly tone: Feb 3.71, Mar 2.94, Apr 3.91. Some call dates disagree with their content.

A separate outage flag on every call (42 touch it), so March splits into touching and not. No date-based before and after.

100 calls, apparently synthetic, no outcomes

One fictional company, repeated score values, no churn or renewal labels.

No synthetic data added. Every trend is directional, and the follow-up list ranks calls for a person. It predicts nothing.

Speaker roles are missing

Nothing says who is the customer and who is Aegis staff.

Side comes from the invitee names, and the Stage 0 model is checked against it. Stage 2 scores the customer apart from staff.

So what

Each trap changed the design: a model call only where metadata cannot decide, and the provided answers held back as a yardstick.
